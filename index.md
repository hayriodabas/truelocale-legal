# TrueLocale

TrueLocale checks the translated screens of your own app. It reads the text on a screenshot and marks what is in the wrong language, cut off or not translated, names the string to fix when the project has your strings file, and reviews several screens together. It also translates a picture into your language on your device.

## Support

Questions, bug reports and feature requests: [open an issue](https://github.com/hayriodabas/truelocale-legal/issues/new).
Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version and the app version from Settings › About.

## Common questions

**Translation says a language needs to be downloaded.** iOS translates on your device and downloads each language once. Connect to the internet, try again and accept the download. You can manage downloaded languages in Settings › Apps › Translate.

**Translation is not available.** On-device translation needs iOS 18 or later. Checks work on iOS 17 too.

**Do I need an account?** No. Translation and the on-device checks (wrong language, placeholders, cut-off and untranslated strings, forms of address, your term list) work without one. Sign in with Apple adds a language model's review of grammar, meaning and tone, 150 checks a day.

**Which strings files can I add?** A String Catalog (.xcstrings), an XLIFF export from Xcode (.xliff) or a .strings file, in Settings › Project › edit a project › Strings file. The file must contain the project's language.

**What is the review?** The checked screens of a project, or of one batch, read together: what is still open on each, where the screens contradict each other, and a verdict. From it you can share a sign-off report (PDF) and a fix list (CSV).

**The camera does not open.** Allow it in Settings › Apps › TrueLocale › Camera, or choose pictures from your library instead.

## Price

TrueLocale is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription.
