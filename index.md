# TrueLocale

TrueLocale checks the text on a screenshot for wrong-language strings, grammar, meaning, slang and tone.

## Support

Questions, bug reports and feature requests: [open an issue](https://github.com/hayriodabas/truelocale-legal/issues/new).
Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version from Settings › About.

## Subscriptions

TrueLocale Pro is an auto-renewable monthly subscription that raises the daily model checks from 10 to 150.
Manage or cancel it in **Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions** on your iPhone. Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
