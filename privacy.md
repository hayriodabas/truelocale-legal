# TrueLocale Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · [Türkçe](#türkçe)

TrueLocale is published by Hayri Odabaş ("we"). This policy explains what the TrueLocale iOS app and its server collect and why.

## What leaves your device
Only the text recognised on a screenshot, together with your target language, tone, app context and glossary, is sent to our server to be checked by the model you chose. Screenshots themselves never leave your device.

## What we store
- Your account identifier from Sign in with Apple, and the email address Apple shares with us (which may be a private relay address).
- How many checks you used each day, to enforce the daily limit.
- Your subscription status, from Apple's signed transaction.
- Per-check statistics: model, target language, score, number of issues and timing.

We do not store the recognised text or the reports. Reports stay on your device.

## Model providers
Text is processed by OpenCode Go and the provider of the model you pick. Muse Spark may use submitted text to train models; the app marks it in Settings. No other model is used for training.

## Purchases
Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We receive Apple's signed transaction to confirm your plan; we never see your payment details.

## Tracking
TrueLocale does not track you across apps or websites and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Infrastructure
Accounts and usage counters are stored with Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt).

## Deleting your data
Settings › Account › Delete account removes your account, usage and subscription records from our server immediately. Checks saved on your device are removed when you delete the app.

## Children
TrueLocale is not directed at children under 13.

## Contact
[Open an issue](https://github.com/hayriodabas/truelocale-legal/issues/new) for any privacy question or request.
